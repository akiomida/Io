How to Play
📱 Move: drag on the left side of the screen (touch), or steer with the mouse, WASD, or arrow keys. You always glide forward, you only steer.
✨ Eat: touch a number. It joins your tail and your body grows longer and bigger.
⭐ Score: eating raises your score. It only drops when you get hit, and every bullet that lands takes 3 points.
💰 Micda: every 100 Skor automatically becomes 1 Micda while you play. Clear badge ranks I–III for bonus Micda. Spend it all at the Micda Shop.
🛡 Shield: tap the SHIELD slot, Shift, or Space. You are immune for 3 s. Blocks bullets and guardian tackles.
🎯 Shoot: press F, right click, or the FIRE slot. Every hit takes 3 points, and walls and ASIMTOTAs wear down shot by shot.
🧵 Ammo: every shot spends one number from your tail (capacity 9, grows to 30 with your form). Eat again to refill.
🛡 Mode: tap the JAGA / FARMING switch above the DASH slot. JAGA locks onto enemies, FARMING locks onto walls.
🌑 Minus hunters: keep your distance from negative numbers. Shoot one to flip its minus into a plus for you.
⚡ Spirit: if your score is 0 and you keep getting hit, your SPIRIT bar drains. Empty means you go down, never out. Eat numbers to recover it.
💬 Chat: tap 💬 once and the keyboard opens right away. Type and hit Enter, and your words float over your head.
⚖ Chat Rules: before the first chat you accept the chat terms. You stay fully responsible for every message you send, and serious violations of the law may be reported.
🌙 Away: the world keeps running when your screen turns off or you switch apps. Attacks can still land while you are gone, so come back soon. A full report waits when you return.
🧱 Walls & Asimtota: shoot walls down to zero and they burst, but ramming through drains your tail, and a dry tail costs points. The Asimtota locks numbers behind its line, topple it to open the gate.
🎡 Prize Spin: open Boost Score on the title screen, share the video, enter the code, then spin for 25 to 500 points. One spin per run.
👑 Badges: clear challenges to raise badges I, II and III and earn Micda for the Micda Shop. Finish all 50 and the Crown of Light lights up above you.
🖥 Mode & Sound: the corners button at the top right turns on Full UX Mode, the whole HUD fades for a clean view, tap ✕ or press Esc to come back. The 🔊 button toggles sound.
